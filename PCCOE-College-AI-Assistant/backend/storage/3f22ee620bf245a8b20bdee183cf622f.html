MESSAGE from Deputy Director: :
Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering is located in an Industrial belt of Automotive Industries and IT hub, of Pune. PCCOE is committed to give Outcome Based Education through Knowledge (K), Skill (S) and Attitude(A), to support skill based technocrats of Naya Bharat. Outcome Based Curriculum, Teaching Learning, Assessment & Evaluation, state of the art laboratory equipments, physical infrastructure and conducive work culture in PCCOE has become first choice for the students to pursue engineering and management education. PCCOE has successfully implemented B. Tech [Computer Engineering] in regional language, a leading step to implement Professional Education in Regional Language, as intended by National Education Policy 2020. Also the Outcome Based Choice Based Credit Framework with Multidisciplinary Minors, Honors and flexibilities will offer multiple choices for students to develop their future career path.

PCCOE has laboratories with the state of art equipments and recognized by many Industries. Collegiate Clubs, Professional student Chapters, Sports, Yoga, NSS, ISR, Art Circle and other support activities will make all round development of students. Conducive research culture supports students to develop research and innovation skills to showcase talents at many platforms like Smart India Hackathon, IEEE Events at National and International level, patent and copyright publications etc. Section 8 company CIIL supports students to register their startup enabling entrepreneur culture. With vibrant Industry Institute Interaction cell and with the support of Training and Placement cell, student got various opportunities for Internships and placements. Industry driven curriculum supports students to become industry ready.

Pune is also selected as one of the smart City in India and thus many Infrastructure developments are coming up with various opportunities to students and faculty.

For continual improvement, PCCOE will be happy to receive input and support from all stakeholders, to enrich in academics, research so that solution to real world problems can be provided.

On behalf of more than 4800 students, 258 Faculty, 145 staff members of PCCOE, it gives me pleasure to invite you to explore the possible interaction to march ahead.


BIOGRAPHY :
Dr Nilkanth Chopade is Professor in Department of Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering, Deputy Director and Dean Administration at Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering (PCCOE), Pune. He has completed Bachelor and Master of Engineering from Government College of Engineering, Amravati and Doctoral degree in Electronics Engineering, from Sant Gadge Baba Amravati University in the year 2009. He has total 33 years of Teaching/ Research/ Administrative experience.

Prof. Nilkanth Chopade has been appointed as Deputy Director & Dean Administration of PCCOE since October 2020. He holds various administrative positions at PCCOE including, Officiating Director, Incharge Principal, Dean Academics, Head of the Department, GB / CDC member, Finance Committee, IQAC Member etc

Prof Chopade is PhD Supervisor in Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU), in Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering and 08 students have completed Ph D degree under his Supervision, at PCCOE Research Centre from Savitribai Phule Pune University(SPPU). Three more research scholars are perusing research under him. His area of Interest is Signal Processing, wireless communication, Antenna Design and Automotive cyber security etc.

To his credit, there are more than 75 research papers in scopus / indexed journals, conference proceedings.Many of research work in which he is associated with received grants, awards and international recognition. He has filed patents, copyrights for the research projects and 04 patents are granted, 06 are published and 09 copyright registered.

To support ‘ATMANIRBHAR BHARAT’ and ‘MAKE in INDIA’, mission of Hon. Prime Minister and to fight covid 19 pandemic, his team have designed and developed 02 products such as, Automatic Hand Sanitizer /Soap Dispenser. Along with Dr. Manish Narkhede he has developed NACHO-Pix software, which is useful for dataset generation useful for algorithm validation. He is actively involved in the review process of many scientific journal articles of International Repute and indexed conferences. He is also working on Automotive Cybersecurity, with International Team of researchers from ARILOU Automation Israel, Can Tho University & Viet Nam.

He has visited many universities from USA, Finland and Malaysia including MIT, Harvard, North Eastern at Boston, Syracuse University, John Hopkins at Baltimore, Princeton at New Jersey, Tampere University, Tampere, LUT, Lappeentranta, Helsinki University, Universiti Technlogi, Mara to study education pattern and practices.

He is expert to Implement NEP 20, Outcome Based Education (OBE), outcome based curriculum design, assessment & evaluation in Autonomous Institutions & universities and invited as expert to talk on different attributes of NEP 20 implementation by many institutions.

He is a member of the Governing Body, College Development Committee, Academic Council and Board of Studies & IQAC of many autonomous institutions in India. Considering the academic & research credibility, he has been invited by many universities as a referee to evaluate PhD Thesis & conduct final viva-voce exams in India & abroad also.

Prof. Chopade is a fellow of various professional societies like IEEE, Institution of Engineers (I), The Institution of Electronics & Telecommunication Engineers (I), Life Member of Broadcast Engineering Society, India, Quality Circle Forum of India & Indian Society for Technical Education, New Delhi. We welcome all the stake holders Students, Academicians, Industry, ALUMNIs to join hands with PCCOE to enrich the conducive work culture to shape the skill based technocrats of tomorrow.
