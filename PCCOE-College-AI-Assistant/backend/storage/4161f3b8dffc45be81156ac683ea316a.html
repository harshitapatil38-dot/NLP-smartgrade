This video provides a step-by-step guide for filling out the PCCOE F. Y. B. Tech. Admission Form.


First Year B.Tech. CAP Admission 2026–27

All students allotted to Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering, Pune (College Code: 6175) through CAP are informed to fill in and take a printout of the ERP Admission Form before reporting physically to the institute.

Step 1: Complete the seat acceptance process (Freeze / Betterment) through your CET login.

Step 2: If you have Freezed the seat, then fill out the ERP Admission Form and take a printout before reporting physically to the institute.

Steps to fill ERP Admission Form (Flowchart)

Login to ERP Pre-Admission Portal (https://learner.pceterp.in/pre-admission)

↓

Enter Application ID (EN26_______) and fetch details

↓

Enter personal email ID → Click SEND OTP → Enter OTP from email

↓

Fill unfreeze details: First Name, Middle Name, Last Name, Mobile Number → Click Admission Confirmation

↓

Check personal email for ERP login credentials (login name & password)

↓

Login to ERP Portal (https://learner.pceterp.in/)

↓

Change default password (enter old & new password)

↓

Click blue circle (top right) → Select User Profile

↓

Fill first four tabs: Personal Details, Contact Details, Family Details, Education Details

↓

Click Print Profile tab to generate form

Boys & Girls Hostel Information

  1. Boys Hostel Capacity: 300 (First Year), allotted on a first-come, first-served basis.
  2. Girls Hostel Capacity: 150 (First Year), allotted on a first-come, first-served basis.
  3. Admission Validity: Hostel admission is valid for one academic year; subsequent allotments are made on a merit basis.
  4. Room Sharing: Each hostel room accommodates a maximum of 3 students.
  5. Facilities Include:
    • Air-conditioned rooms
    • 24×7 electricity and water supply
    • High-speed Wi-Fi
    • Solar-powered hot water system with geysers as backup
    • Well-equipped gymnasium and common recreational areas
    • Doctor on call
    • Daily newspaper
    • Housekeeping services
  6. Mess Fees Include: Tea, breakfast, lunch, and dinner.
  7. Hostel Contact Details:
    New Boys Hostel: 020 - 2760 0142 | Girsl Hostel: 020 - 2760 0146
