Functions of the Governing Body

Subject to the existing provision in the bye-laws of the respective College and Rules laid down by the State Government / Parent University, the Governing Body shall -

  • Guide the institute while fulfilling the Objectives for which the institute has been granted Autonomous Status.
  • Institute Scholarships, Fellowships, Studentships, Medals, Prizes, Certificates and academic approvals on the recommendations of the Academic Council.
  • Approve new Programs of Study leading to Degrees.
  • All recruitments of the teaching faculty / Principal / Director Shall be made by the Board of Governance/State Government as applicable in accordance with the policy laid down by the UGC and State Government from time to time.
  • To approve the Annual Budget proposed by the Finance Committee of the institute before submitting the same to the AICTE / UGC.
  • Perform such other functions and Institute Committees as may be necessary and deemed fit for proper development of the Institute. Where academics Council differs on any proposal of the board of studies, it will have the right to return the matter for reconsideration to the Board of Studies concern or reject it, after giving reasons to do so.

Notes:

  1. 1. Term of nominated Members shall be Five Years from Academic Year 2023 - 2024 to 2027-2028.
  2. 2. The Board of Governors shall be reconstituted every Five Years except in the case of UGC Nominee.
  3. 3. Director shall convene a Meeting of the Board of Governors at least twice in a year.
